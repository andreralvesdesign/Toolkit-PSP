Before you start

Risks that are not always visible

Psychosocial risks are aspects of work that can negatively affect physical, emotional and social health.

They can be present in workload, leadership, relationships, organisational culture or the balance between professional and personal life. Although they are not always visible, their effects build up and can compromise wellbeing, performance and staying in work.

Individual factors that influence the perception of risks

Although risks may be present in the same work context, their impact varies from person to person.

This difference depends not only on organisational conditions, but also on individual factors that influence how each person perceives, processes and responds to stress.
