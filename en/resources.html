Internal resources

We Are Sonae

A multidisciplinary support programme developed by Sonae in 2013, aimed at supporting employees and their families in moments of greatest need and mental, economic and social vulnerability. It is run by multidisciplinary, specialist teams from the Portuguese Red Cross (CVP), and the privacy of those who use it is guaranteed.

Mental health and psychological support

A mental health programme in partnership with the CVP and its psychosocial support network, which includes accredited clinical psychologists.

  • Free and confidential support plan: up to 10 consultations, over 12 months, which may be extended following a clinical assessment of the case.
  • Format: consultations can be in person or online, and the format will be chosen together with the psychologist.
  • Coverage: all employees!

How to access

Call the dedicated line of the We Are Sonae Programme, run by the CVP and available 24 hours a day.

Hidden

When you call, you only need to identify yourself as an MC employee and you will be directed to the network of accredited clinical psychologists.

Multicare Health Insurance

Through Multicare Health Insurance’s Online Medicine service, permanent employees have access to completely free psychology consultations (by phone or video call), as well as wellbeing programmes.

Wellbeing programmes

  • Sleep Better
  • Get Fit Programme
  • Smoking Cessation Programme

Plan and formats

  • Free and confidential support plan: psychology consultations and wellbeing programmes.
  • Format: unlimited, if by phone or video call; 6 in-person Psychology/Psychiatry consultations per year.
  • Coverage: all permanent employees with health insurance!

Online Medicine · Health Insurance

Besides the mental health initiatives, Health Insurance’s Online Medicine service provides completely free access to a range of specialist consultations, conveniently and without the need to travel:

  • General and family medicine consultations
  • Prescription of tests and medication
  • Dermatology
  • Nutrition
  • Women’s health consultations
  • Among others…

We Are Sonae

This programme aims to support employees and their families, in situations of social and economic vulnerability, at the moments they need it most. In partnership with the CVP, it works across different areas (in addition to the mental health strand):

  • Socio-economic support
  • Legal support
  • Oral health programme, in partnership with “Mundo a Sorrir” (Lisbon, Porto and Braga)

Support for victims of domestic violence

  • Free and confidential helpline (hidden), available 24 hours a day
  • The CVP works with its victim support structures, which are part of the National Network for Support to Victims of Domestic Violence, with prior consent
  • Comprehensive support: legal and psychological
  • Socio-economic support: whenever considered necessary following analysis of the case and adjusted to its characteristics

The support plans ensure the necessary confidentiality and respect the sensitivity inherent in each case.

Occupational Medicine

At MC, all employees have access to Occupational Medicine consultations, in addition to the compulsory periodic check-ups:

  • Occasional Examination at the Employee’s Request
  • Occasional Examination at the Physician’s Initiative
  • Occasional Examination at the Company’s Request

These consultations can be requested through the ‘People’ team in stores, operations, logistics or the central structure. In some smaller stores, access to a consultation is not always possible on the same day or within a few days of being referred.

It is important to know

If Occupational Medicine cannot respond within the expected timeframe, an appropriate alternative should be activated (e.g. the We Are Sonae Line hidden – 24h, psychological support or an external resource), so as not to compromise a timely response to the employee.

A final word

Looking after mental health at work does not require perfect answers or clinical training.

Above all, it requires a willingness to observe, listen and connect people to the right support. Small gestures – an attentive question, a safe space to talk, the courage not to ignore the distress of the person beside us – make all the difference.

Psychological First Aid is human, close and practical support for someone who is struggling – in a way that always respects their dignity, their culture and their abilities. It does not replace clinical care, but it is often the first step that makes the difference.

Keep this material as a resource you can come back to whenever you need it. And remember that looking after others starts with looking after yourself too.

Thank you for being part of building a more attentive, safe and healthy working environment for everyone.
