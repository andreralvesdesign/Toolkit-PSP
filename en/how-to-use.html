What this material is

A practical guide to Psychological First Aid at work

It helps you recognise signs of psychological distress, in yourself, in a colleague or in your team, and respond in a humane, safe and structured way, following a simple logic:

  1. 1Observe
  2. 2Listen
  3. 3Link to the right support

It does not require clinical training. It brings together information, checklists, flowcharts, videos (role plays) and exercises, so you always have what you need to hand in every situation.

Who it is intended for

For all employees and leaders

Whatever your role or area. It requires no clinical experience: it was designed precisely for those who, in everyday work, may be the first person to notice that something is not right.

For those who lead

Tools to identify signs early and act safely.

For every employee

Ways to look after yourself and to support a colleague in distress.

What is asked of you is not technical knowledge, but availability, attention and respect for others.

What it does not replace

Psychological First Aid is a first line of support

  • It is not psychotherapy, clinical counselling or diagnostic assessment
  • It does not ask you to analyse what happened or to solve the person’s problem
  • This material does not replace professional psychological support, medical care or the organisation’s internal procedures

Its aim is to help you be present and to link the person to the right support.

In situations of risk, turn to Part 6 or, in an emergency, call 112.
